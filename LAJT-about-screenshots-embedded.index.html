Today

Building for funders & Federation

This is infrastructure. It should last.

I didn't set out to build an app. I set out to stop losing track of Shabbat dinners.

Our community's event life was already rich — it just lived across dozens of WhatsApp threads, Instagram stories, and email blasts that only the most plugged-in people ever saw in full. I kept watching genuinely great programming reach a fraction of the people who would have loved to be there.

"The goal was never to replace the group chat. It was to give it a front door."

LAJT is still, at its core, an engineering project built by someone inside this community. Every feature exists because a real person needed it, not because it looked good on a roadmap.

— Andrew, Founder

I'm a first-time solo founder building LA Jewish Tonight from inside the community it serves. I bring years of hands-on community organizing and an existing grassroots network; early support would help turn a proven community need into durable shared infrastructure.
